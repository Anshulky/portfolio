## Landing Page — What to Keep vs. Move

The landing page should work as a fast pitch (~15 seconds to place who you are), with everything deeper pushed one click away.

**Keep on landing page:**

1. **Identity block (hero)** — photo, name, location, and a 1-line role tag (e.g. "Research Staff, KCDH IIT Bombay · Member of Technical staff, Radai") *before* any prose.
2. **Short bio** — 2–3 sentences max: role + what you work on. No specific techniques, no funders.
3. **Contact/links row** — Email, GitHub, CV, LinkedIn, Scholar, ORCID. Already good, keep prominent.
4. On the right hand side add a cool backdrop like vit working. with the tabs being jusy above. upon clicking the selected content should appear with nice animation.

**Move OFF landing page:**

- Research-interests paragraph (architectures, interpretability, self-supervised learning, etc.) → About page.
- Funders paragraph (Koita Foundation, Oraibio, Wadhwani AI) → About page

## Point-by-Point (from professor's feedback)



### 1. Too much text on landing page

Cut the 3-paragraph bio down to one tight paragraph (2–3 sentences). Move the "research supported by" line to an About section.

Suggested trimmed bio:

> I'm Research Staff at the Koita Centre for Digital Health (KCDH), IIT Bombay, and a Master's by Research student in Healthcare Informatics under Prof. Kshitij Jadhav. Alongside this, I work as a member of technical staff at Radai Pvt. Limited, a medical AI startup building clinical imaging tools.

Flip current ordering (student-first) to lead with the Research Staff role, since it reads as more senior. (Reflected in the rewrite above.)
